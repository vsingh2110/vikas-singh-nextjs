- No text, no words, no captions baked into the image. No logos or watermarks.
- Cinematic, respectful, National Geographic documentary tone; natural film grain.
- Keep hero and OG under 300 KB (JPG). Aim under 400 KB for figures. 1200x630 for hero/OG; landscape figures 1600x900; portrait figures 1200x1500.
